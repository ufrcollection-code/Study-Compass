Privacy & your data

Updated 21 November 2026 · Web version 1.0

A private space to study

Study Compass does not require an account. This version contains no advertising, analytics SDKs or third-party scripts. We do not send your tasks, exam dates, notes or focus history to a server.

Data on your device

Your study content, completed focus sessions and preferences are stored in your browser’s local storage. The service worker caches app files for offline use. This data is not encrypted by the app. Other people with access to your device or browser profile may be able to access it.

Backups and deletion

You can export your data as a JSON file and restore it in Settings. Keep exports somewhere private. Settings → Delete all study data removes study records and resets preferences. Clearing this website’s browser storage also removes its local data and cached files. Uninstalling an installed website alone may not remove browser storage.

Website hosting

Your hosting provider receives normal web requests, which may include your IP address and browser details. If this app is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub’s privacy statement applies to hosting-related processing: GitHub privacy statement.

Device permissions

This web version does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts or notification access. File selection is initiated by you when restoring a backup.

Android versions and changes

This policy describes the included web application. An Android wrapper, advertising, analytics, cloud sync or other integrations may change how data is handled. The developer must update this policy and the store disclosures to match the released app.
